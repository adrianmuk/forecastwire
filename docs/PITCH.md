# ForecastWire presentation video

Target length: 2–3 minutes. Record a clear voiceover with four simple visuals: a plain prediction post, ForecastWire's market and capture form, a real receipt, and the product's next-step diagram. This is a pitch, so spend more time on the problem and distribution than on clicking through every button. Replace bracketed facts with your own verified information before recording.

## Draft script and shot plan

**0:00–0:30 — Problem.** Show a simple post: “I think this event will happen; YES is 37¢.”

> People share predictions with market prices in chats and posts. Hours later, that price has changed. A screenshot does not show the live market, and a plain market link does not show what the price was when the person made the call. That makes the discussion hard to revisit.

**0:30–1:15 — Product.** Show a real Panta market, choose YES or NO, add a short note, and open the receipt.

> ForecastWire gives a public prediction a point in time. You choose a side on an open Panta market, add context if you want, and publish a receipt containing the market question, your call, both quoted prices, and the time our server fetched them. Anyone with the link sees that captured quote next to the current Panta price and how the chosen side moved. No wallet is needed to make or read a receipt.

**1:15–1:45 — Why Panta and Solana.** Show a live brief, a reported trade link, and a compact diagram or README section.

> Panta supplies the live prediction markets, market details, spot prices, trades, and public positions. ForecastWire uses those data in the receipt and updates the comparison whenever it is opened. A server-side signature detects edits to a receipt link. The signature is issued by ForecastWire; it is not an independent on-chain record of the past price. Market identifiers and reported trade transactions lead back to Solana.

**1:45–2:25 — Distribution and vision.** Show a receipt link in an example post; do not show fictional usage figures.

> We are starting with people who already make public YES/NO calls in communities. The first distribution test is sharing the live product in Panta and Colosseum builder communities and asking whether people can make and revisit a call without guidance. [Insert actual feedback or usage only after it happens.] The next step is a better way to display a person's history of calls and market changes, if early users want it.

**2:25–2:45 — Founder and close.** Show the project logo, live URL, and team names.

> I'm [name and accurate one-sentence background]. We built the MVP during the hackathon, and the live demo and source code are available at [URLs]. Our goal is to make a public prediction easier to check later, when the market has moved.

## Recording checks

- Replace any price and event example with a real market visible in the app. Do not present an unchanged market as having moved.
- State your actual team background and development history. Do not claim users, revenue, or demand without evidence.
- Keep the presentation under three minutes. Put a working URL and project name on the final visual.
- If the Free Render service has slept, open it and wait for it to load before recording.
